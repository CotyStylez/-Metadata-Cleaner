Enterprise Privacy Guarantee & Architecture

1. Zero Cloud Uploads: Every byte of your photos is processed strictly inside your device's browser memory (HTML5 Canvas 2D engine). At no point are images, filenames, or metadata transmitted over the network.

2. Mathematical EXIF Destruction: Rather than attempting to patch or mask headers, ExifZero decodes raw image pixels into memory and reconstructs a completely fresh graphic file with pristine binary headers, eliminating all APP1/EXIF segments, GPS markers, IPTC records, and camera serial numbers.

3. Air-Gapped Operation: You can disconnect your device from the internet or run this workstation on private corporate intranets. The application functions 100% offline.

4. No Cookies or Telemetry: No tracking pixels, user session cookies, or telemetry analytics are deployed in this software.
